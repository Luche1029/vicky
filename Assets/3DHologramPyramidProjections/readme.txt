This asset creates projections for any 3D-model into 3D-holographic pyramid in real time. 

After installing this asset, open the demo scene "DemoScene" and run it to see how this asset works.
To see real 3D-hologram you need to put a special 3D-pyramid on the screen of your device (mobile phone or tablet). 3D-pyramid can be made by your hands. To make 3D-pyramid you need: a transparent cover from box for CD-disc, narrow adhesive tape, ruler, pencil, A4 sheet, paper cutter or buy the same pyramid in any online store. Instructions for made by your hands are inside this asset.

This asset contains a folder ModelsContainer with box-model into it. In this folder you can place your models.
	You can look hologram of your model /left-right-front-back sides/ into the pyramid. Put your 3d-model into ModelsContainer-folder in this asset and disabled box-model. Run scene "DemoScene" and then put a special 3D-pyramid on the screen of your device (mobile phone or tablet). After that you can see a real hologram of your model. 

/Folders/
Pictures: - png images
Scripts:  - RotateObjects.cs script in C# that rotate objects
              - screenSetUp.cs in C# that manage object's sizes
Sounds: - audio mp3 demo file 

This asset can be used for any devices: mobile phones and tablets.
This asset will be work good for Unity 2017, 2018, 2019
